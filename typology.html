Decoupling typology

Each burden-tier cause is classified from three signals comparing the first period (1995–99) with the last (2020–24): S, the relative change in its share of the 14-area publication pool; B, the relative change in its share of the seven-cause DALY pool; and A, the change in log2 ABR. The signed protocol fixed the type names, definitions and the three-signal framework; anything matching no rule is reported as unclassified, never forced.

Sensitivity behaviour: nasopharyngeal and oral cancer are technology-ignited under all three threshold sets — the only two fully robust assignments. Otitis media is resolution-dilution under the main and low sets and unclassified under the high set. Hearing loss is constant under the main and high sets and unclassified under the low set; its type describes dynamics only and must be read together with its level — ABR 0.36 in 2020–24 (log2 ABR −1.47), the pool's deepest deficit. Of the three causes unclassified under the main thresholds, laryngeal cancer becomes resolution-dilution under the low set, other pharyngeal cancer becomes burden-tracking under the low set, and thyroid cancer becomes constant under the high set. Each sits near a rule boundary; the classification reports that rather than resolving it by fiat.

How to read this page

B is a share, not a level. A falling B means relative dilution inside the seven-cause pool. Annualised absolute DALYs in fact rose for all seven burden-tier causes between the first and last periods (+3.9% to +101.3%), so "resolution" in the resolution-dilution label means relative share dilution only; nothing on this page claims any burden fell in absolute terms.

Thresholds. The signed protocol did not fix numeric thresholds. The thresholds used here — main ±25% for S and B and ±0.5 for A, with the ±15%/±0.3 and ±35%/±0.7 sensitivity sets shown for every assignment — were operationalised at the typology stage, before the classification was run. The typology is therefore a descriptive classification under pre-stated type definitions, not preregistered inference.

Type names are shape labels, not mechanism claims. "Technology-ignited" names a configuration — publication share rising while burden share is flat or falling, with ABR rising — and asserts nothing about what caused it.
