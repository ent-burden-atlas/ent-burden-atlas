What this is

The ENT Burden Atlas presents a registered-protocol census of how research attention in otolaryngology (ear, nose and throat medicine) has aligned with disease burden over 30 years. Publications were counted in Europe PMC for 14 disease areas (1995–2024; 357,711 area-assigned records, corresponding to 329,250 unique publications); for the seven areas with a matched Global Burden of Disease (GBD) 2023 cause, output is set against DALY burden (1995–2023). Four measurement layers are reported on one scale: publication-share dynamics, the attention–burden ratio (ABR), the geographic distance between burden and research centroids, and a rule-based decoupling typology. All values on this site use the corrected search formulations of Amendment v1.2 (21 November 2026); see the correction note on the Methods page. The protocol was signed on 8 November 2026, before production began, and publicly registered on 10 November 2026, after the initial counts and the first record-level retrieval (OSF registration, DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/NUC4B).

Headline findings

  • Shares rotated towards oncology. The five head-and-neck cancers jointly rose from 30.7% to 38.5% of the pool, driven by oral cancer (5.1% → 10.3%) and thyroid cancer (11.0% → 14.5%); nasopharyngeal cancer was nearly flat (5.8% → 6.4%), and laryngeal cancer moved against its own group (4.7% → 1.9%).
  • Otitis media shrank in share, not in output. Its share fell from 9.2% to 3.3% while its five-year output grew from 2,229 to 3,706 papers — a denominator effect.
  • Hearing loss is the central level finding. It carries 72.7% of the seven-cause DALY pool against 31.6% of the same pool's publications, and the gap widened over 30 years (log2 ABR −0.95 → −1.20).
  • Geography converged in direction for most causes, not in level. The burden–research centroid distance fell for six of the seven burden-tier causes (−14% to −80%); nasopharyngeal cancer moved away, from a close start (780 → 1,601 km, +105%). Last-period distances remain 1,081–5,957 km.
  • Typology: otitis media is resolution-dilution (a relative share dilution — absolute DALYs rose for all seven causes); oral cancer is technology-ignited under all three threshold sets; hearing loss is constant in dynamics (under all three sets) at a deeply negative level (ABR 0.43); nasopharyngeal, laryngeal, other pharyngeal and thyroid cancer are reported as unclassified, not forced into a type.

A manuscript reporting this census is in preparation (it has not been published). The frozen tables on the Downloads page can be reused under the archive licence; the pipeline is specialty-agnostic and can be re-run for another specialty's disease pool.

Scope boundary: every quantity here describes the configuration of published research relative to modelled burden estimates. Nothing on this site ranks diseases by clinical importance or states how much research any disease should receive.
