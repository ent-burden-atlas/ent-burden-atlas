About this atlas

The ENT Burden Atlas is the open resource layer of a preregistered census of research attention versus disease burden in otolaryngology, 1995–2024. It exposes the census's frozen results — publication shares, attention–burden ratios, burden–research centroid distances and the decoupling typology — by area, period and country, for reuse and for replication of the design in other specialties: the pipeline is specialty-agnostic in structure (a frozen disease pool, a public bibliographic source, a public burden source, and the four measurement layers above).

Provenance

Preregistration: Open Science Framework project 42ytu; registration DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/NUC4B. The protocol was registered before record-level production began; Amendment v1.1, substituting GBD 2023 for the GBD 2021 release that the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation had retired, and Amendment v1.2 (21 November 2026, search-formulation correction) are filed with the registration.
Frozen archive: Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24418492 — the original frozen snapshot: all original tables, the record-level country-parse table, the GBD input extracts, all production and figure code, and the project documents. The corrected tables are on this site's Downloads page and in the OSF project's frozen-archive folder.
Canonical site: https://burden.erbihou.org
Frozen as of: 12 November 2026; corrected basis frozen 21 November 2026 (Amendment v1.2). This is a static site generated from the frozen result files; it is not updated as new data appear.

Correction note (21 November 2026). A pre-submission audit found that two of the originally frozen search formulations (nasopharyngeal and other pharyngeal cancer) used anatomy terms alone, without a cancer limiter, and were contaminated by non-cancer records (sampled PPV 0.333 for nasopharyngeal cancer in 2020–2021). Every value on this site now uses the corrected, cancer-limited formulations of Amendment v1.2; the original formulations are retained in the archive as a sensitivity basis. See the correction note on the Methods page.

Data sources

  • Publications: Europe PMC — frozen title/abstract queries, counts for 1995–2024, and record-level retrieval for the geographic layer.
  • Burden: Global Burden of Disease 2023 estimates (Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation) — DALYs primary, prevalence as the sensitivity calibre — for the seven matched causes, 1995–2023.
  • Country classification: World Bank income classification (snapshot frozen 12 November 2026 and held fixed across all periods); country centres from the frozen snapshot of the mledoze/countries dataset.
